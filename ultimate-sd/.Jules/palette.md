
## 2025-05-24 - Added aria-labels and titles to Blog Editor toolbars
**Learning:** WYSIWYG editor toolbars often consist entirely of icon-only buttons, making them a critical area to verify for `aria-label` and `title` attributes for accessibility and usability. Screen reader users would have no context for these actions.
**Action:** Always check toolbar components, like rich-text editors or formatting panels, for missing accessible labels on icon buttons.
